The thesis
Power Grid is the cheapest quality infrastructure stock in India right now. At 16.6x PE and 4.75% dividend yield, you're being paid to wait for the rate cut (October MPC). The company has ₹1,95,000 crore of projects in the pipeline — essentially a decade of visibility. When the RBI cuts rates, regulated utilities like Power Grid re-rate automatically because their DCF value rises. This is as close to a risk-free entry as you'll find in equity markets.
⚠ Risk: Right-of-way delays and rate cut being pushed further (if crude spikes again) could delay the re-rating. Crude staying above ₹85 = rate cut delayed = Power Grid re-rating delayed. Not a growth story — a value + income story.
